# tokenAtlas name and brand usage

The **tokenAtlas** name, the project's distinctive logo/wordmark, and the
**K_NODE** name and visual identity identify the official project. This
document describes the maintainer's intended rules for using those identifiers
*as brands*. It does not assert that any mark is registered.

Section 6 does **not** grant a general license to trade names, trademarks,
service marks or product names. The code license permits commercial use,
modification, redistribution and independent products; this brand policy is
not an additional restriction on those code rights.

This document does not revoke copyright permissions already granted for any
individual artwork or software file, replace third-party licenses, or limit
rights available under applicable law. Project-owned SVG artwork is provided in

grant an independent product official status or replace third-party font terms.

## Uses that are welcome

- Identify the original project truthfully, for example:
  "Based on tokenAtlas by K_NODE — https://tokenatlas.dev/".
- State that a fork is based on tokenAtlas, or that another tool is compatible
  with tokenAtlas, when this is accurate.
- Use the name in documentation, articles, reviews and fair/descriptive
  references to the original project, including reproduction of required
  attribution notices.
- Link to the official website or repository without implying endorsement.

These examples are not exclusive; applicable legal rights remain unaffected.

## Uses that need separate permission

Please request permission before using the official tokenAtlas or K_NODE logo
as the identity of a different app, paid service, company, website or domain,
or creating branding that may confuse users into thinking an independent fork
is the official project. Do not claim that K_NODE endorses, certifies, sponsors
or maintains an independent product without consent.

If you distribute a separately branded fork, use a clearly distinguishable
product identity and make its independence clear. **Keep the applicable
LICENSE, NOTICE and copyright notices** under Apache-2.0 even after
rebranding. Removing branding does not remove attribution obligations;
retaining attribution does not grant official status.

An unmodified redistribution must likewise not misrepresent who operates,
supports or endorses it.

## Permission and corrections

For ordinary brand-permission requests, contact the maintainer through the
official project repository or **dev@tokenatlas.dev**.
Do not publish credentials or sensitive materials in an issue. The public
contact route is also published on tokenatlas.dev. No trademark registration is
asserted by this document.
